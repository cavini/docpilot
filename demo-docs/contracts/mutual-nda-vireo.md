# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 4 February
2026 between **Lumen Payments Inc.**, of 1100 Market Street, Wilmington,
Delaware, and **Vireo Labs, Inc.**, of 88 Orchard Way, Austin, Texas. Each Party
may disclose information to the other, so each is both a "Discloser" and a
"Recipient" depending on which way the information is travelling.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a possible integration between Lumen's payments
platform and Vireo's inventory system (the "Purpose"). Each expects to learn
things about the other that are not public, and this Agreement sets out what may
be done with them.

## 2. What is confidential

**2.1** "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by a Discloser to
a Recipient in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential
from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

**2.2** It includes, without limiting 2.1: source code, system architecture,
security practices and findings, pricing, customer lists, transaction volumes,
roadmaps, and the existence and contents of the discussions between the Parties.

**2.3** Information disclosed orally is confidential whether or not it is
confirmed in writing afterwards. A Discloser is not required to send a follow-up
note to protect something it said in a meeting.

## 3. What is not confidential

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can
show:

- was public at the time of disclosure, or became public afterwards other than
  through a breach of this Agreement;
- was already in the Recipient's possession without a duty of confidence;
- was received from a third party free to disclose it; or
- was independently developed by people who had no access to the Discloser's
  Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations

**4.1** A Recipient will use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, and
will not use it to compete with the Discloser or to develop a substantially
similar product.

**4.2** A Recipient will protect Confidential Information with at least the care
it uses for its own confidential information, and in no case with less than
reasonable care.

**4.3** A Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its employees,
contractors, and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are
bound by obligations at least as protective as these. The Recipient remains
responsible for what those people do with it.

**4.4** A Recipient will not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble
anything disclosed to it, except to the extent that restriction is unenforceable
by law.

## 5. Compelled disclosure

A Recipient required by law, by a court, or by a regulator to disclose
Confidential Information may do so, provided it gives the Discloser as much
notice as the law permits, discloses only what it is required to disclose, and
cooperates at the Discloser's expense with any effort to limit the disclosure.

## 6. Term

**6.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and either Party may end it on
**30 days** written notice.

**6.2** The obligations in Clause 4 continue for **three years** after the date
of each disclosure, and ending this Agreement does not end them.

**6.3** For anything that is a trade secret under applicable law, the
obligations continue for as long as it remains a trade secret, which may be
longer than three years.

## 7. Return and destruction

Within **15 days** of a written request, a Recipient will return or destroy the
Confidential Information in its possession and confirm in writing that it has
done so. A Recipient may keep one copy in its legal files, and copies held in
routine backups that it does not separately retrieve, in each case subject to
Clause 4 for as long as they are kept.

## 8. Residual knowledge

A Recipient's employees may use, in the ordinary course of their work, general
knowledge, skills and experience retained in unaided memory from exposure to
Confidential Information. This clause does not permit the use of anything
written down, nor the deliberate memorisation of Confidential Information in
order to rely on this clause.

## 9. Non-solicitation

For **12 months** from the date of this Agreement, neither Party will solicit
for employment any employee of the other who was introduced to it in connection
with the Purpose. A general advertisement not directed at that person is not
solicitation.

## 10. No licence, no obligation

**10.1** Nothing here transfers ownership of, or grants a licence to, any
intellectual property. All Confidential Information remains the Discloser's.

**10.2** Nothing here obliges either Party to disclose anything, to proceed with
the Purpose, or to enter any further agreement.

**10.3** Confidential Information is provided as is. Neither Party warrants its
accuracy or completeness.

## 11. Remedies

Money may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and a
Discloser may seek an injunction or other equitable relief in addition to any
other remedy, without having to post a bond.

## 12. General

**12.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and
the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

**12.2** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written
consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

**12.3** This Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties on its
subject. An amendment is effective only in writing, signed by both.

**12.4** If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest continues in force.

**12.5** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, and an electronic
signature is as effective as one on paper.
