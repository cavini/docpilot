AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 is made on 12 September 2026 between Lumen Payments Inc.
("Lumen") and Halcyon Retail Ltd ("Customer"), and amends the Master Services
Agreement between them dated 1 March 2026 (the "Agreement"). Terms defined in
the Agreement have the same meaning here.

The Parties have agreed to raise the availability commitment and to shorten the
notice required to end the Agreement, in exchange for an increase in the
platform fee. Accordingly the Parties agree as follows.

1. Clause 6.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

   "6.1 Uptime commitment. Lumen will make the Platform's payment capture and
   authorisation endpoints available 99.95% of the time in each calendar
   month, measured as the proportion of one-minute intervals in which a
   well-formed request received a response other than 5xx."

2. Clause 4.3 of the Agreement is amended by replacing "90 days" with "60
   days", so that either Party may now terminate at the end of the Initial
   Term, or of any renewal period, on at least 60 days written notice.

3. Clause 3.1 of the Agreement is amended by replacing "USD 7,500 per month"
   with "USD 8,900 per month". The transaction fee of 0.4% of Processed Volume
   is unchanged.

4. The cap on service credits in Clause 6.2 is raised from 30% to 40% of the
   month's platform fee. The rate of 10% of the platform fee for each full
   0.1% below the commitment is unchanged.

5. This Amendment takes effect on 1 October 2026. The change to Clause 3.1
   applies from the first invoice issued on or after that date, and the change
   to Clause 6.1 applies to availability measured in October 2026 and in each
   month after it.

6. Everything else in the Agreement is unchanged and remains in force. Where
   this Amendment and the Agreement disagree, this Amendment prevails.

Signed for and on behalf of Lumen Payments Inc. and Halcyon Retail Ltd by their
authorised signatories on the date first written above.
